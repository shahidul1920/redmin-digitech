### **Shipping Management**

Our Shipping Management System removes the complexity of international importing, offering secure and reliable freight from China to Bangladesh. Redmun Digitech supports two streamlined workflows tailored to how you do business:

* **Full Product Sourcing (Order Request):** Ideal for customers who need end-to-end purchasing support. Simply submit a product link or image, and we will find the supplier, verify the best price, purchase the item, perform quality inspections, handle customs clearance, and deliver it directly to your door.  
* **Logistics Only (Create Booking):** Designed for customers who have already purchased their goods. Send your packages to our China warehouse, where we will verify the shipment, calculate weight, manage the Air or Sea freight, clear Bangladesh customs, and complete the final local delivery.  
* **Streamlined Portal:** Customers can easily log in to submit shipment details, generate shipping marks, track booking statuses, and pay shipping charges all in one place.
